# Future Agent Spec: Traffic Intelligence Agent

## 1. Purpose & Objectives
The **Traffic Intelligence Agent** tracks organic distribution channels, inbound backlink opportunities, and high-affinity niche communities suited for specific utility toolkits.

---

## 2. Strategic Functions
1. **Niche Distribution Mapping**:
   - Matches specific utilities (e.g. *Dice Roller* or *D&D tools*) with relevant subreddits (`r/dnd`, `r/rpg`), Discord communities, and blog roundups.
2. **Backlink & Mention Discovery**:
   - Identifies non-commercial educational articles and developer documentation listing broken or outdated utility alternatives and suggests UTL.tools replacements.
3. **Keyword Expansion**:
   - Discovers long-tail search intent variations to enrich page metadata and header structures.
