# Future Spec: Productivity Packs & Custom Collections

## 1. Concept: Utility Bundles
Productivity Packs are purpose-built curated collections of utilities tailored for distinct professional workflows.

---

## 2. Standard Curated Packs

### 💻 Developer Pack
- JSON Formatter & Validator
- Base64 Encoder / Decoder
- UUID / GUID Generator
- Unix Timestamp Converter
- URL / URI Encoder & Decoder
- AI Token Counter

### 💼 Business & Founder Pack
- Free Invoice Generator
- QR Code Generator
- Professional Email Signature Generator
- Business & Startup Name Generator
- Discount & Margin Calculator
- Percentage Calculator

### 🌐 Sysadmin & Network Pack
- What is My IP
- DNS Records Lookup (DoH)
- Ping & Latency Tester
- User Agent Inspector
- Browser & System Info
- Screen Resolution Checker

### 🎓 Student & Writer Pack
- Word & Character Counter
- GPA Calculator (4.0 Weighted/Unweighted)
- Chronological Age Calculator
- Random Picker & Decision Wheel

### 🎨 Creative & Design Pack
- Color Code Converter (HEX/RGB/HSL)
- Aspect Ratio Calculator
- Screen Resolution Checker
- QR Code Generator

---

## 3. User-Created Shareable Collections
Users will be able to bundle any 3 to 10 utilities into a public or unlisted URL (e.g. `utl.tools/p/team-onboarding-kit`) to share with colleagues or students.
