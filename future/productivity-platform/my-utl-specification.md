# Future Spec: "My UTL" Personal Utility Productivity Platform

## 1. Overview
"My UTL" is the Phase 3 evolution of UTL.tools, transforming the public utility library into a customizable, unified digital workstation for individual professionals, developers, and founders.

---

## 2. Core Capabilities
1. **Zero-Friction Authentication**:
   - WebAuthn Passkeys / Magic Link email login.
   - Fallback offline local storage synchronization.
2. **Pinned Toolbox Dashboard**:
   - Users can pin their 6 to 12 most-used utilities onto a single personalized start page (`utl.tools/my`).
   - Split-screen workspace mode allowing dual-tool side-by-side workflows (e.g. JSON Formatter on the left, Base64 Encoder on the right).
3. **Saved Presets & Defaults**:
   - Save frequently used company details into the Invoice Generator.
   - Save default signature formats into the Email Signature Generator.
   - Save preferred currency, tax rates, and rounding preferences globally.
4. **Tool Chaining / Pipelines**:
   - Pipe the output of one tool directly into another (e.g. Generate UUID -> Encrypt / Encode -> Create QR Code).
