## 2. Agent Architecture Foundation
Future autonomous subagents powered by the Sensor Fabric:
1. **Discovery Agent**: Scans web hubs for emerging utility demand and search queries.
2. **Traffic Agent**: Monitors first-party analytics and competitor traffic shifts.
3. **Search Agent**: Monitors SERP positions, snippet changes, and keyword intent.
4. **Competitor Agent**: Tracks feature releases, pricing, and domain changes of competitor sites.
5. **Social/Community Agent**: Audits discussions on Reddit, GitHub, and Twitter.
6. **Geography Agent**: Identifies country-specific and language-specific demand opportunities.
7. **Trend Agent**: Detects mathematical anomalies, traffic spikes, and seasonal trends.
8. **Intelligence Analyst**: Synthesizes multi-domain signals into fused intelligence.
9. **Recommendation Agent**: Ranks actionable opportunity queues for human review.
10. **Validation Agent**: Audits data provenance, schema shifts, and sensor health.
